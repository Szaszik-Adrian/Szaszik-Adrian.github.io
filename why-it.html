Why ICT suits me

I enjoy building applications and seeing my ideas work. I have learned programming for about four to five years. In SilverMozi, I worked on the C# desktop application. I want to improve my development and testing skills.

Why I chose HZ

I chose HZ for its practical education and projects. I want to build on my previous experience. Working on projects and receiving feedback can help me improve how I design and test software.

After graduating, I want to work as a junior software developer. I also want strong testing skills. Later, I want to take responsibility for larger applications and the quality of my work.
